# Privacy

logcatdaily tools is a small script I run on my own Mac to read stats for my own YouTube channel and Instagram account (logcatdaily).

It only reads my own channel's data: views, watch time, and like and comment counts. It doesn't access anyone else's account, doesn't collect data about viewers, and doesn't share anything with third parties.

Tokens stay on my machine. Nothing is sold or published.
